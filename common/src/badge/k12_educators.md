# Citizen Science for K-12 Educators
## Award Requirements
To earn the Citizen Science for K-12 Educators badge, participants must:

- Create or log in to a SciStarter account.
- Successfully complete the [Foundations of Citizen Science](https://scistarter.org/badges/6Jg_IwmfQTaJ2kUNePsO5Q) training and earn the corresponding badge.
- Successfully complete the Citizen Science for K-12 Educators training module.
- Visit and engage with the resources in the SciStarter Education Hub.
- Build a SciStarter list of citizen science projects for their classroom.

****
## Learning Outcomes
Earning the Citizen Science for K-12 Educators badge signifies that the recipient has successfully demonstrated proficiency in the following learning outcomes:

- **Explain** the role of citizen science in advancing scientific research and public engagement.
- **Apply** citizen science practices to strengthen teaching and learning across educational settings.
- **Use** the SciStarter Project Finder to identify citizen science projects aligned to classroom goals, grade levels, student interests, and local contexts.
- **Identify** which citizen science experiences are appropriate to different grade levels and developmental stages.
- **Integrate** citizen science into multidisciplinary learning experiences across content areas.
- **Create** and organize a classroom-ready collection of citizen science projects using SciStarter Lists to support student participation and investigation.
- **Facilitate** student participation in citizen science through SciStarter accounts and dashboards to support reflection, documentation, and sustained scientific engagement.
- **Transform** schoolyards and community spaces into living laboratories for observation, investigation, and discovery.
- **Participate** in two citizen science projects and reflect on classroom applications.

## About SciStarter
SciStarter is a global citizen science hub that connects millions of people to thousands of opportunities to participate in real scientific research. We help people turn curiosity into Acts of Science through a powerful project and event finder, personalized dashboards, and tools that make participation easy across projects, websites, and apps. Led by the SciStarter Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and supported by a mission-aligned LLC for client services, SciStarter uses evidence-based approaches that are field-tested and scaled to support participants, scientists, and facilitators while coordinating major initiatives such as Citizen Science Month and Do NASA Science LIVE.
