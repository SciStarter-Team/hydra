Certified Ambassadors

Certified Ambassadors have completed the Foundations of Citizen Science training and badge and the Ambassador training and badge. They’ve also been screened by a third party and submitted an application to commit to being part of this searchable directory. Please adhere to your organization’s screening protocols when inviting an Ambassador to attend an event. SciStarter is not liable or responsible for any interaction once you connect with an Ambassador.
