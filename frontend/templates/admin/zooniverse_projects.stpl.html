Zooniverse Projects

The Zooniverse projects whose classifications are credited to SciStarter projects. A project connected here offers its visitors the Zooniverse username box, and is listed by the finder’s Zooniverse affiliate filter.

Each project is read as the Zooniverse account its team made a collaborator. Project leaders are given SciStarter’s account; projects connected before November 2026 are read as Daniel Arbuckle’s. Panoptes gives an account that isn’t a collaborator empty pages rather than an error, so a project is only connected once Panoptes confirms that its account can read it.
