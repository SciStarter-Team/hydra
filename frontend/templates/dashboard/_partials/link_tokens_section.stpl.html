Link Tokens

Link Tokens are a way of connecting your SciStarter account to affiliate projects, so that you can see your contributions to those projects here on your dashboard. Not all affiliates use Link Tokens, since SciStarter provides several ways of connecting affiliates. If an affiliate asks you for a Link Token, you can create and manage it here.
