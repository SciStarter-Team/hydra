Your Link Token

Your Link Token connects your SciStarter account to trusted SciStarter Affiliate projects, so your participation is recorded on your SciStarter dashboard. If a project's website or app asks for your Link Token, go to your SciStarter Dashboard, look for the Link Token, click "Copy" and then paste it where instructed on the project's website or app. This is a once-per-project setup. Moving forward, your participation in that project will be automatically credited on your SciStarter dashboard. To disconnect one project, use the disconnect option on that project's website or app, if it offers one. To disconnect every project at once, click "Delete" next to your Link Token on your SciStarter dashboard. You'll get a new token right away, and any project you want to keep will need it pasted in again.
