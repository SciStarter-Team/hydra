Citizen and Community Science Library Network Sign-Up Form

The Citizen and Community Science Library Network is open to all libraries—and community-based organizations—to support public engagement in real scientific research. Through ready-to-use field-tested professional development, program and event templates, kit-building guides, customizable resources, and a network of peers, members of all capacities are better equipped to create and sustain community hubs for participatory science!

As a member of the network, you’ll receive:

  • Exclusive access to trainings and webinars
  • Newsletters with updates, ideas, and opportunities
  • Access a supportive Facebook group of peers
  • Practical tips, tools, and technical assistance
  • Connections to libraries, volunteers and organizations across the country

Membership is free. When you sign up, your library will be added to an interactive map to help others find you and for you to connect with other participating libraries. Join today and be part of a growing movement connecting science, libraries, and communities!
