SciStarter is for Everyone

Volunteers

Discover diverse projects that offer opportunities to use your hobbies and interests to engage in hands-on activities that contribute to real scientific research.

Project Leaders

SciStarter helps project leaders recruit and retain volunteers, with tools to share updates, recognize contributions, and engage with their community.

Facilitators & Organizations

SciStarter enables organizations to design and promote impactful community engagement campaigns with a distinctive array of resources, including tailored microsites and corporate social responsibility programs.

Researchers

SciStarter equips researchers with valuable data and insights into the behaviors and trends of the citizen science community, supporting the development and advancement of this emerging field.
