Lists from Zoom registrations

Choose one or more CSV files exported from Zoom. A person who registered for several sessions appears once, with every session listed.

People who were rejected, failed screening, or are outside the countries the program covers. Left out unless this is ticked.

How registrants are matched

  • A registrant counts when the email address they gave Zoom belongs to a SciStarter account, as any of the addresses it signs in with. Capitals don't matter.
  • Ambassadors are people whose application has passed, the same people as in the Ambassador export.
  • Champions who are not Ambassadors have finished the Citizen Science Champion training and are not Ambassadors. The status column says how far each has got.
  • Registrants who used an address SciStarter doesn't know are on neither list, even if they have an account under another address.

Zoom's registrant list for a meeting or webinar and its registration report both work, as long as they are saved as CSV. A CSV file without an Email column is searched for email addresses instead. Those addresses may include people who did not register, such as a host, so their rows say to check them.
