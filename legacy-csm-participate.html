Participate in Real Research During Citizen Science Month

Every contribution during April 2026 counts toward 2.50 Million Acts of Science!

Anyone can do Acts of Science by participating in a citizen science project or event. You can participate on your own, with friends, or as part of a live or local gathering.

No experience is required.

Start in Minutes

You can participate in any of the ways below.

All count toward the shared goal of 2.50 Million Acts of Science.

Join a Citizen Science Project

Participate in projects about nature, space, health, climate, and history. Anytime, anywhere.

Attend virtual or in-person events hosted by libraries, museums, schools, and community groups.

Whether you participate for five minutes or five hours, every Act of Science counts.

Your participation helps reach the collective goal.

  • Participation in SciStarter Affiliate Projects is tracked automatically. SciStarter Affiliate projects are marked with a logo.
  • Coming soon: Report any Act of Science using the Report Acts tab. You do not need an account on SciStarter to use the Report tool.

Sharing your experience helps inspire others and social media posts count as Acts of Science. Post a photo using #ActsofScience to appear on the Wall of Fame.

Participants who participate in and report Acts of Science are eligible for prizes and recognition during Citizen Science Month.
